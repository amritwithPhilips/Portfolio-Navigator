Assessment
logic		 How each lever works Entry criteria and funding mechanism differ per lever. Programmes must qualify before budget is allocated. ✔ Verified LFR Named Law, Regulation or Fiscal ruling for 2027. Verified = Legal / Tax / Compliance has confirmed the obligation is real, applies to Philips, and scope is minimum-viable. Unverified or gold-plated claims are excluded. ✔ Confirmed carry-over Signed Business Case that started spend in 2026, within <10% baseline margin. ROI-ranked = ordered by benefit-to-cost ratio within envelope. 100% bucket = all carry-overs fully protected regardless of ROI. ✔ BU-prioritised CCBs Mandatory upgrades, patches, small continuity items (CCBs). 52% bucket = funded to 52% of total sustaining demand; BU IT leaders choose which CCBs enter. 40% bucket = tighter ceiling, more budget freed for value. 100% bucket = full demand funded. ✔ Merit-ranked · Auto ceiling Programmes ranked by composite merit score (benefit strength, ROI, strategic alignment, evidence quality). Auto ceiling = the engine funds programmes in merit order until the 100M total budget is consumed. No manual cap — the budget itself is the ceiling. Highest-benefit programmes are guaranteed entry before lower-ranked ones. ✔ Value-Based · Decommission scope Programmes from the Value Based Portfolio that meet the Decommission and Replatforming scope criteria, prioritised by merit within this lever. Activated only in selected scenarios. Savings generated by retiring legacy platforms are factored into the programme's net benefit score. Hard Benefit
