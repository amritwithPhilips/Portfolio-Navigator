ExCo decisions customization and validation

What changed

The ExCo view now supports leader-specific Programs decisions, a Demand / Hard Benefits chart toggle, additional funding previews and custom columns from normalized and original source data. It shares the same scenario as the six-tab workbench. v5.7.0, v5.8.0 and both source workbooks are preserved.

  1. Decisions: select an ExCo leader, adjust inclusion or review request, merit and funded scope. Use Company funding settings for the same budget, levers and scoring configuration available in tab 2.
  2. Funding opportunities: enter an additional budget and rationale, choose both streams or F&R/BU Led, preview the added Programs and recipient costs, then apply or cancel.
  3. Customize columns: search and select scenario or original EW/BU source fields. Choices are stored per leader in the saved scenario. Fork and portable scenario recovery retain them.
  4. Synchronization: edits made in ExCo appear in the workbench, and workbench edits appear in ExCo. ExCo leader filters synchronize across Programs views. Charts and leader filters do not independently change funding.

Calculation and decision boundaries

Additional funding uses the current scenario request, reviewed merit, source eligibility and confirmed carry caps. It sorts eligible remaining scopes by priority stage, descending merit, ascending request and ID. It skips scopes that do not fit the entered extra budget. The company envelope increases by the entered amount; unused extra remains headroom.

Applying a top-up retains prior funding amounts across the company. This prevents a higher global budget from silently refilling other portfolios. Those retained automatic decisions are visible overrides. Use the return-to-automatic preview to release them, or Undo to restore the entire previous decision. New manual decisions and source refresh rules continue to apply.

Productivity claims are a savings proxy, shown separately from revenue and soft claims. All remain submitted lifetime claims. The model does not convert them into annual cash savings or assume an incremental benefit when an already funded scope receives more funding. Source benefit evidence stays eligible for prioritization.

Company settings affect all leaders. Leader and chart filters affect the displayed Programs, while receiving-organisation costs include the full company. Operations remains source-fixed. Limits and traceability

The model remains an offline scenario tool. Finalised is a local editing lock, not a centrally authenticated approval. Saved custom layouts belong to the scenario and carry into its fork; a fresh scenario starts with default layouts. There is no new quantified delivery-capacity or dependency solver. Desktop Chrome was tested at 1366px and 1920px; other browsers and mobile workflows are outside this suite.

Original source columns retain their source units. All finance and funding computations continue in M€. Source workbook hashes, protected build hashes, source-linked exports and the publication receipt are retained in the release evidence folder.

The suite corrected two harness assumptions during validation: saved-view checks now wait for persisted state to load, and numeric input comparisons use numeric values. A final UI transition check is recorded in the browser evidence.
