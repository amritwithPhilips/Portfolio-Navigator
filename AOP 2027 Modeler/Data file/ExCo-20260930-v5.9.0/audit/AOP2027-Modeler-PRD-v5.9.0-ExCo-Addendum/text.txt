AOP 2027 ExCo workspace product requirements  
Version 5.9.0 adds leader-specific decisions, funding opportunities and customizable evidence to the ExCo 
view. This addendum supplements the v5.8.0 ExCo specification and the unchanged v5.7.0 detailed modeler 
specification. Both earlier HTML builds and source workbooks remain unchanged. 
Leader decisions 
The Decisions workspace supports all base Programs priorities, with PMI retained separately on top. Leaders 
can include or exclude selected Programs, return them to automatic funding, and review requested 
investment, merit and partial scope. Every funding change is previewed before apply. Reviewed merit is 
optional; a blank value uses the calculated score. Rationale is required for individual reviews. 
Charts and evidence 
The decision map toggles between requested Demand and quantified Hard Benefits against merit. Bubble size 
reflects requested investment; colour distinguishes funded and not funded. Clicking or pressing Enter on a 
bubble opens source evidence. Hard Benefits excludes soft benefit claims. 
Custom columns include normalized scenario fields and original EW and BU source columns. Leaders can 
search, add or remove fields while Programs identity and source access remain visible. Layouts are stored 
separately by leader in the saved scenario, retained through reload, fork and portable export. A fresh scenario 
uses default layouts. 
Shared company state 
ExCo leader selection synchronizes the Programs filters in the detailed workbench. Request, merit, inclusion 
and funding edits work in both directions. All six views and exported finance ledgers use the same current 
result. Receiving-organisation cost allocation remains company-wide, distinct from accountable Programs 
ownership. 
Company funding settings expose the base budget, tolerance, merit floor, all five score weights, editable 
priority lever bases and optional IT Simplification. These controls affect all leaders and are explicitly labelled 
company-wide. Preview shows the resulting company and recipient costs.

Additional funding calculation 
The user enters a positive additional company envelope, one ExCo leader, a stream or both streams, and a 
rationale. The planner uses current scenario requests, merit and source eligibility. It ranks remaining eligible 
scopes by priority stage, then descending merit, ascending request and ID. It selects whole remaining scopes 
that fit the additional envelope. 
Confirmed carry-over remains capped at the approved baseline. Explicit manual exclusions and held Programs 
are not selected automatically. The added envelope increases the company budget by the entered amount. 
Any unused extra remains headroom. Existing allocations across all leaders are retained, so increasing one 
leader’s funding does not silently refill other portfolios. 
Retained automatic allocations become explicit recorded overrides. A separate preview can return those 
retained decisions to automatic, while Undo restores the entire previous top-up transaction. The existing 
finalisation and changed-source decision holds continue to apply. 
Benefit interpretation 
Preview reports newly funded productivity claims as a savings proxy, separate revenue claims and separate 
soft claims. All are submitted lifetime claims. The model does not infer annual cash savings or incremental 
benefits from additional funding of an already funded partial scope. Missing quantified evidence is not 
converted into a realised return. 
Validation and finance continuity 
The release validates 32 existing scenarios, 144 leader and stream combinations at €1M, €5M and €15M extra 
budgets, source-linked Excel exports and independent workbook replay. The final QA report contains assertion 
counts, source reconciliation, UI checks and the tested additional-funding example.
