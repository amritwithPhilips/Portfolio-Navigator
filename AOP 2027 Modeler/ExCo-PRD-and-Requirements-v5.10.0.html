AOP 2027 · v5.10.0 PRD and requirements

This addendum supersedes conflicting ExCo interface and carryover-threshold text in v5.9.0. Source finance allocation rules remain unchanged. User request: recreate the build for the five missed workflows.

IDRequirement / acceptance
EXCO510-01Carryover ROI in Tab 2
Restored 0–10x slider and numeric input in 0.5x steps. Confirmed baselines are exempt; 10x is baseline-only, 0x applies no ROI filter to other eligible carryovers. Default 10x preserves current Optimal funding.
EXCO510-02Scenario Excel downloads
Visible current/saved revision selector, complete/inclusions/filtered scope and download next to saved revisions in Tab 2. Filenames identify scenario, revision and scope. Selected Programs and Scenario Selection sheets make scope explicit; full finance evidence is retained.
EXCO510-03Overall State navigation
Overall State replaces Company position and the all-leaders dropdown label. Compare options and its recommendation navigation are removed from ExCo.
EXCO510-04Visible funding decisions and sorting
My portfolio, Decisions and Funding opportunities provide Opt in, Opt out, Partial funding, Automatic and request/score review. All changes preview before apply. Sort by Hard Benefits, requested budget or score in either direction; unknown hard benefits remain last.
EXCO510-05One shared bubble chart
ExCo calls the same renderer as Tabs 3, 4 and 6: logarithmic amount scale, merit axis, funding/Decision Band colors, legend totals, demand/Hard Benefits toggle and source detail interactions. Axis and color synchronize both ways.

Finance rule contract

Default 100 M€ budget, ROI 10x baseline-only, confirmed baseline caps, unchanged priority ordering, source decision eligibility and merit calculation. At 0–9.5x, unconfirmed carryovers pass when their quantified benefit/request ratio meets the threshold; 0x removes this ratio test. Missing ratio fails positive thresholds. Confirmed baseline eligibility is unaffected by the threshold. Engine and preview settings are persisted and included in Excel.

Decision contract

Opt in funds the current request; Opt out funds zero; Partial funding requires an amount above zero and below the request; Automatic removes the manual override. These are manual scenario decisions, with rationale, preview, one event, undo and finalised-state protection. They preserve source data and share the same result with the workbench.

Export contract

Export the selected saved snapshot without activating or mutating it. Solve that snapshot with its own data, settings, overrides and reviews. Include scenario ID, name, revision, scope, exact selected rows and full financial/source evidence. Filtered scope uses current selected leaders and filters against the selected snapshot.

Compatibility and evidence

Prior v5.9 carry settings had no effect. Missing carryPolicy is therefore interpreted as baseline-only until the user operates the new control. This prevents silent funding drift on portable imports. New sessions use carryPolicy roi-v1 and 10x. Test evidence: 2237 assertions, 53 configurations and source-workbook replay.
