Key findings and remaining data limitations

  • Default Optimal remains 99.963 M€ base funding, 5.190 M€ PMI and 470.314463 M€ Operations, totaling 575.467463 M€.
  • Approved EW unallocated share remains 22.6303317536%. The 40 M€ charging key remains excluded from demand.
  • Confirmed carryovers use min(request, approved baseline). Other eligible carryovers use the restored ROI filter. Source conditions, manual overrides, tolerance and whole-Programs fit still apply.
  • ROI means submitted lifetime benefit divided by scenario request; it is not an annual cash return. Hard and Soft claims remain separate.
  • Existing v5.9 portable scenarios retain baseline-only eligibility until the ROI control is changed, preventing an old unused 0x setting from silently changing allocation.
  • Saved scenarios are browser-local. Use portable JSON to open scenarios saved in another browser or previous build; then download that scenario from this build.
  • Operations remains fixed. Funding changes recalculate the same source-linked receiving-organisation ledger in ExCo, all six tabs and Excel.
  • No known blocking defect remains in the tested workflows. Original workbooks and v5.7.0–v5.9.0 are preserved.
