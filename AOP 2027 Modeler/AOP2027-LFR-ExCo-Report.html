LFR Lever — How It Works

The LFR lever controls the budget envelope reserved for all mandatory legal, regulatory, and compliance programmes (Stage 0)

LFR stands for Legal, Financial & Regulatory. These are non-negotiable programmes — failing to fund them creates direct legal exposure, regulatory breach, audit findings, or loss of operating licence. The LFR lever does not make them optional; it controls how much of the overall AOP budget is ringfenced to cover them.

Lever basis options:

  • Bucket % (default 82.4%): Reserves a % of total Stage 0 demand. At 100%, all verified LFR programmes are funded. Reducing below 82.4% will start dropping the lowest-scoring unverified LFR items.
  • AOP % basis: Reserves a % of the Total AOP budget (e.g. 40% of 100M = 40M for LFR). Useful when you want to cap LFR regardless of how many programmes are submitted.
  • Fixed amount (M€): Sets a hard ceiling in euros. If LFR demand exceeds this, lowest-scoring programmes are dropped.

Funding priority within Stage 0: (1) Verified LFR — programmes with both LFR Name and LFR Year evidence fields populated. (2) Unverified LFR — sorted by score descending. (3) CCB exceptions — always funded regardless of score. Changing the lever % only affects which unverified programmes fall outside the envelope; P1 Immediate programmes are always at the top of the order.

Hold conditions in LFR: A programme is held (not funded automatically) if: priority = "TBD", overall decision band is invalid or blank, or a Contain condition has no remarks/justification. Holds require source workbook correction or an explicit ExCo override — they cannot be resolved by adjusting the lever.
