# Decision policy

## Evidence and cost basis

Use IT Investment Requested 2027. Source workbooks express requested investment and numeric benefits in K€; divide by 1,000 for M€. Do not substitute handshaked, updated or previously prioritized budgets. Preserve those values as comparisons if provided.

Numeric benefits are total lifetime benefits. Carry-over benefits are original business-case claims. Extract only an explicit, unambiguous amount and retain the quoted text, source unit and period. Ranges, annual-only values without a lifetime total, historic realised benefits, phrases such as “significant savings” and unclear amounts remain unresolved. Above-five-times claims remain visible and cannot drive automatic benefit-led allocation. Exactly five times is permitted. Protected obligations, Change and Sustain and evidenced hygiene do not require quantified benefits.

No assumed completion percentage, remaining return, sunk-cost protection, revenue margin, discount rate, delivery probability, new financial benefit or source-independent risk rating is permitted.

## Stages and overlap

1. Review waste. Hold discretionary work with severe findings pending a reviewed viable scope. For protected obligations, retain priority and show the scope challenge.
2. Protected obligations: include weighted validity exactly 5; validated Immediate must-do matrix records; clearly named requirements with a compliance year; the explicit Finance planning allocation. A high risk tier alone with validation “NO” is insufficient. Order Immediate work by deadline severity, then exposure; retain the other protected requests. Use the selected coverage percentage.
3. Change and Sustain: fund the selected percentage of its eligible request base after protected overlaps. This is pooled capacity, so proportional funding is permitted. Reduced funding is not proof that the original outcomes remain deliverable.
4. Regions: North America, Greater China and International Regions. The allowance is a percentage of the total envelope. Deduct earlier regional allocations before additional demand. Regional requests cannot re-enter another stage to evade this ceiling. If protected demand already exceeds the allowance, show the excess and permit no additional regional funding.
5. Carry-over: prefer otherwise eligible work with lifetime benefit at least the configured multiple of the 2027 request, initially two times. Do not award points for money already spent. Work below that preference threshold can still compete in the common pool if otherwise eligible.
6. Hygiene: ring-fence decommissioning in both reporting groups, technical debt reduction and lifecycle work. Require specific source evidence and defined scope. Distinguish optional enhancement from the essential work. Prioritize stated consequences of inaction, then lower requested investment. Previously protected hygiene consumes the reservation. Preserve unused hygiene capacity unless leadership explicitly releases it.
7. Common demand: eligible residual demand competes together. Fund whole affordable programs. Skip an unaffordable request with a reason. No invented percentage cuts outside pooled capacity, protected coverage or a reviewed scope decision.

Hygiene eligibility is identified before carry-over selection so the reservation cannot be consumed by discretionary carry-overs. Every request has one funding allocation and one primary stage. Organisation and purpose are additional reporting classifications, not additional allocations.

## Mandatory scoring

Validity weights: named authority/instrument 25%; mandatory requirement 30%; confirmed applicability 25%; independent validation 20%. Multiply each 1-to-5 rating by its weight. Missing criteria mean Unknown, not zero or fully validated. A weighted score of exactly 5 is automatic priority.

Use the recorded validated Immediate must-do determination from the matrix. Retain the source ratings and detect any disagreement with calculated risk. Exposure = compliance severity × likelihood, on 1–25: Low 1–4, Medium 5–9, High 10–14, Critical 15–25. Immediate means critical exposure or deadline under six months. Scheduled means high exposure or six-to-twelve-month deadline. Remaining work follows its compliance window. These ratings never become risk scores for unrelated demand.

Minimum-viable-scope ratings run from 1 (substantial optional scope) to 5 (only the mandate). Below 4 requires scope challenge. Funding a smaller amount does not certify compliance; show the unfunded obligation until a delivery decision resolves it.

## Waste scoring

Nine ratings, each 1 healthy to 5 worst: sunset technology 20%; target architecture 15%; enterprise duplication 10%; weak business case 15%; strategic fit 15%; fragmentation 10%; unused capacity 5%; lock-in / exit costs 5%; deferrability 5%.

Use the weighted sum only when all nine ratings exist. Bands: below 2 Healthy; 2 to below 3 Contain; 3 to below 4 Challenge; 4 to 5 Exit candidate. A maximum individual rating of 5 triggers a specific review regardless of the average. Validated legal, regulatory, security and quality obligations are never rejected on waste score alone. Unknown assessments remain Unknown. Preserve source overall decision and remarks alongside the calculation.

## Merit score

The four dimensions total up to 100 points:

| Dimension | Initial points |
|---|---|
| Lifetime benefit / requested investment, maximum 50 | Missing or below 1: 0; 1 to below 2: 20; 2 to below 3: 30; 3 to below 5: 40; at least 5: 50 |
| Submitted priority, maximum 25 | Must Do 25; Must Have 18; Deprioritised 5; missing/unrecognised 0 |
| Consequence, maximum 15 | Missing/uncontextualised number 0; general consequence 5; named affected activity and explicit harm 10; that consequence with a stated percentage or enterprise reach 15 |
| Waste, maximum 10 | Healthy 10; Contain 6; Challenge 2; Exit or Unknown 0 |

Read Impact and Cost of Not Doing first. Problem Statement and Scope can support the consequence tier when they explicitly state the effect of inaction or end of support. Preserve the passage and mark interpretation for review. Dependencies identify prerequisites; they add no monetary value or separate points. The reference engine uses explicit text patterns as an assist, not an independent assessment of truth.

For regional, carry-over and common demand, require a usable benefit strictly above the 2027 request, a claim no greater than five times, the scenario's merit floor, relevant waste evidence and resolved prerequisites. Sort eligible work by absolute usable lifetime benefit descending, then benefit/request multiple descending, then merit descending, then stable source reference. The displayed merit line is an eligibility threshold, not the actual funding cut-off. This is deterministic ranking under constraints, not a proof of optimal economic value.

## Levers and presets

All percentage controls allow direct numeric entry. Envelope starts at 100 M€. Protected coverage, Change and Sustain coverage and reservations accept 0–100%. The denominator for Change and Sustain is its remaining eligible requested investment. Regional and hygiene percentages use the total envelope. Carry-over threshold initially 2; merit floor initially 45. Weights sum to 100; individual tier points may be configured within each reference scale. Changing a lever produces Custom settings, without silently resetting another lever.

Applying a preset retains reviewed decisions and clearly says so. Provide a separate action to clear them.

## Manual decisions and financial integrity

Require a reason for inclusion, exclusion or changed amount. Above-five-times benefit-led inclusion requires an explicit review acknowledgement. Severe findings require a scope acknowledgement. A reviewed amount must be positive and no greater than the request; exclusion uses zero. Per-program protected percentages map to the same amount. Retain protected priority and show any remaining obligation.

Manual decisions must propagate to grouped totals, both reporting-group tabs, total portfolio, reservation use, executive summary and scenario state. Manual requests that cannot fit remain pending with an explanation. Never silently overspend or alter another program's requested amount. A mandatory shortfall caused by the envelope stops later automatic allocations. Source changes hold affected manual decisions for re-review.

Requested = allocated + requested-but-unfunded. Envelope = allocated + unallocated. Unallocated may contain unused ring-fenced hygiene and therefore differs from freely available money. Business-led + enterprise-wide = portfolio. Stage totals = portfolio. Usable benefit subtotal includes only full funded requests with unflagged claims above one and up to five times the request. It is not a cash-equivalent subtotal.
