# Annual Operating Plan 2027
## 1. Investment priorities and funding order

**Recommendation.** Protect mandatory obligations, maintain essential change capacity and reserve funding for technology health. Allocate the remaining budget to eligible programs with the strongest submitted business value. Business-led and enterprise-wide demand compete within one portfolio. Their funding totals are reporting views, not separate budget ceilings.

The current demand file contains **279.995 M€** of requested investment. Under the recommended Balanced investment scenario, **94.260 M€** is allocated to **134 requests**, leaving **5.740 M€** available. All amounts are in millions of euros, shown as M€. Calculations use the requested 2027 investment and retain the original submitted benefits.

| Order | Definition and funding decision |
| --- | --- |
| Preliminary waste review | Assess whether the proposed spend adds avoidable cost, duplication or complexity. Severe findings require a scope decision. A validated legal, regulatory, security or quality obligation remains protected from rejection on waste score alone. |
| 1. Protected obligations | Work required to meet legal, fiscal or regulatory obligations. Automatically prioritise fully validated obligations and validated Immediate must-do demand from the waste matrix. Include clearly named requirements with a compliance year and the Finance exception below. Apply the selected funding percentage before other allocations. |
| 2. Change and Sustain | Recurring platform capacity for essential changes and continued operation. Fund the selected percentage of eligible demand after removing protected obligations. A reduction represents a capacity decision and requires an assessment of the service impact. |
| 3. Regional demand | Demand owned by North America, Greater China and International Regions. The regional allowance covers these organisations across all funding stages. Deduct their earlier allocations before selecting additional regional programs on merit. |
| 4. Carry-over commitments | Work started before 2027. Give earlier consideration to eligible programs whose original lifetime benefit is at least twice the 2027 request. Past expenditure alone does not justify continuation. Programs below this threshold may still compete in the common demand pool. |
| 5. Technology hygiene | A ring-fenced allocation for decommissioning, technical debt reduction and technology lifecycle work across business-led and enterprise-wide demand. Require a stated retirement, support, maintenance or technical debt issue and a defined scope. Prioritise the stated consequence of inaction, then lower requested investment. New features bundled into replacement work need a separate scope decision. |
| 6. Common demand pool | All other eligible demand competes for the remaining envelope. Select whole programs in the stated merit order. Explain requests left below the funding line, including those that do not fit the remaining budget. |

**Finance treatment.** Allocate the full **6.225 M€** Finance Change and Sustain request to protected legal, fiscal and regulatory demand for this planning round. This is an explicit planning decision. It does not establish that every activity in the request has been independently validated.

**Guidance still required.** Keep Group Functions **7.630 M€** and Supply Chain **4.160 M€** in Change and Sustain until their mandatory and discretionary scope has been separated. Neither request receives additional mandatory funding for the same scope. When the split is confirmed, move the agreed amount between the two stages and recalculate the remaining allowances.

**One allocation per request.** Funding is recorded once, at its first qualifying stage. Regional ownership and hygiene purpose remain visible as reporting classifications. They do not create another funding entitlement. Identify hygiene work before selecting carry-overs and preserve its reservation until the hygiene stage.

## 2. Mandatory obligations and waste assessment

**Validity establishes the obligation.** The four supplied criteria measure the strength of the mandate. Each is scored from 1, an unsupported assertion, to 5, a fully substantiated requirement. Multiply each rating by its weight and add the results. The resulting validity score remains on a 1-to-5 scale.

| Validity dimension | Weight | Executive definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named authority and instrument | 25% | The specific law, regulation or tax rule, its relevant clause and the issuing authority can be identified and checked. |
| Mandatory requirement | 30% | The proposed action is required to meet the obligation. Optional improvements are distinguished from the required response. |
| Confirmed applicability | 25% | Legal, Compliance or Tax has confirmed that the requirement applies to the relevant entity, country and process. |
| Independent validation | 20% | An accountable control function has reviewed the requirement and recorded its conclusion independently of the sponsor. |

**Automatic priority.** A calculated validity score of **5** receives automatic priority. So does a request recorded as **validated and Immediate must-do** in the matrix. An Immediate risk rating without a validated mandate does not qualify on that rating alone. A clearly named requirement with a compliance year also qualifies for protected planning allocation, with its validation status shown separately. Missing or conflicting evidence remains visible for resolution.

**Risk determines sequence.** Severity measures the harm from non-compliance. Likelihood measures how probable that harm is without action. Both use the supplied 1-to-5 ratings. Multiply them to calculate exposure: **Low 1-4, Medium 5-9, High 10-14, Critical 15-25**. The deadline is the time remaining to comply. Immediate priority applies to critical exposure or a deadline under six months. Scheduled priority applies to high exposure or a deadline within six to twelve months. Other obligations are planned within their compliance window. Within the Immediate group, fund the nearest deadlines first, then the highest exposure. These ratings apply only to compliance demand.

**Scope determines what should be funded.** The minimum-viable-scope rating runs from 1, substantial optional scope, to 5, only what the obligation requires. Ratings below 4 require a scope challenge. Record the priced minimum when available. A funding reduction leaves a visible obligation gap until the owner confirms a compliant delivery plan.

**Waste measures avoidable spend.** Each condition uses its own supplied rating descriptions: **1 means the condition does not apply; 5 means it fully applies**. Multiply the nine ratings by their weights and add them. A waste rating of 5 requires a scope review.

| Waste dimension | Weight | What the assessment tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunset technology investment | 20% | New spending on technology already designated for retirement or replacement. |
| Departure from target architecture | 15% | Growth outside the agreed future technology estate. |
| Duplicate enterprise capability | 10% | Local spending on a capability already available from a shared solution. |
| Weak business case | 15% | Insufficient benefits, accountability or evidence of payback. |
| Low strategic fit | 15% | Lack of a traceable connection to a stated business or company priority. |
| Fragmentation | 10% | Repeated standalone changes that could be consolidated into planned work. |
| Unused capacity | 5% | Additional licences or capacity despite spare capacity already available. |
| Supplier dependence and exit cost | 5% | Spending that increases dependence on a supplier or system intended for exit. |
| Deferrable demand | 5% | Work that can wait without a dated business, legal or security consequence. |

**Decision bands.** Below 2: Healthy, proceed on merit. From 2 to below 3: Contain, proceed with conditions. From 3 to below 4: Challenge, require a priced minimum scope. From 4 to 5: Exit candidate for discretionary work. Any individual rating of 5 triggers a specific scope review even when the average is low. For protected obligations, retain priority and challenge the proposed solution. Missing ratings remain Unknown and are never treated as healthy.

## 3. Program merit and funding levers

**Program merit measures the strength of discretionary demand.** Protected obligations, Change and Sustain, and evidenced hygiene do not require a quantified financial benefit to enter their funding stages. Regional, carry-over and common-pool programs must have a usable benefit greater than the 2027 request and meet the selected minimum merit score.

| Merit dimension | Maximum points | Definition and initial scoring rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benefit relative to investment | 50 | Submitted lifetime benefit divided by requested 2027 investment. Below 1 time or missing: 0 points. From 1 to below 2 times: 20. From 2 to below 3 times: 30. From 3 to below 5 times: 40. At least 5 times: 50. Exactly 1 time does not pass the funding requirement. |
| Submitted business priority | 25 | The sponsor's stated priority: Must Do 25, Must Have 18, Deprioritised 5, missing or unrecognised 0. A Must Do label alone does not establish a mandatory obligation. |
| Consequence of not proceeding | 15 | Use the stated impact, problem and scope: no usable consequence 0; general consequence 5; named affected activity and explicit harm 10; that consequence with a stated percentage impact or enterprise-wide reach 15. Preserve the supporting passage. Dependencies identify prerequisites and add no separate points. |
| Waste assessment | 10 | Healthy 10, Contain 6, Challenge 2, Exit or Unknown 0. The waste decision overrides the points. Unknown means unassessed, not confirmed waste. |

**How the score affects selection.** Add the four dimensions, up to 100 points. The proposed minimum is 45 for Balanced investment and 65 for Confirmed evidence. Among eligible programs, rank by usable absolute lifetime benefit, then benefit relative to the request, then total merit score. Use the source reference to resolve a final tie. A high score cannot remove an evidence hold, override a reservation or justify overspending.

**Benefit discipline.** Claims above five times the request remain visible but require review and manual inclusion before benefit-driven funding. Ambiguous values are held for clarification. Do not estimate savings from descriptive language. Carry-over benefits remain the original business-case values and may include benefits already realised. This comparison is not a calculation of remaining investment return. Exactly five times is eligible if the underlying amount is clear.

**Worked example.** Submitted lifetime benefit of **7.628 M€** divided by requested investment of **2.950 M€** equals **2.586 times**. Benefit points 30, submitted priority 25, stated consequence 15 and Unknown waste 0 produce **70 points**. The source passage states a 7-10% productivity loss. This supports the consequence tier but does not create an additional monetary benefit.

| Lever | Definition and effect |
| --- | --- |
| Portfolio envelope | Total available investment, initially 100.000 M€. Every allocation reduces this balance. Business-led and enterprise-wide totals show the resulting distribution. |
| Protected funding coverage | Percentage of protected requested investment to allocate, initially 100%. Apply it to automatically prioritised obligations, including Immediate must-do work. Lower coverage preserves priority and displays the unfunded obligation and required delivery decision. |
| Change and Sustain coverage | Percentage of eligible Change and Sustain demand after mandatory overlaps. The revised base is 22.921 M€. Finance is excluded from this base. |
| Regional allowance | Maximum total investment for regional demand, expressed as a percentage of the envelope. Earlier regional allocations consume this allowance. Additional regional programs cannot bypass it through another stage. |
| Hygiene reservation | Percentage of the envelope reserved for evidenced decommissioning, technical debt and lifecycle work. Deduct qualifying hygiene work funded earlier. Keep unused capacity reserved until leadership explicitly releases it. |
| Carry-over threshold | Minimum lifetime-benefit multiple for earlier consideration of work already started, initially twice the 2027 request. All other evidence and waste rules still apply. |
| Minimum merit and waste evidence | The minimum program score and whether a completed waste assessment is required for discretionary selection. Score tiers and weights are configurable policy choices. |
| Reviewed inclusion and allocation | Add or remove a named program, or enter a reviewed scope amount, with a recorded reason. Recalculate all stages, reservations and reporting totals. Retain any obligation gap or unresolved dependency. |

Percentages support sliders and direct entry. Selecting a scenario sets its stated values. Editing a value creates a Custom scenario. Refreshes retain source values, approved decisions and their reasons, and identify evidence that has changed.

## 4. Recommended scenarios and allocation effects

**Balanced investment - recommended.** Retain mandatory funding and current Change and Sustain capacity, preserve the hygiene reservation and fund eligible business opportunities. Allow missing waste assessments to remain visible as conditions for review. This provides the broadest supportable investment plan within the current evidence and selected reservations.

**Confirmed evidence.** Apply a higher merit threshold and require a completed waste assessment for discretionary programs. Use this scenario when leadership wants to limit recommendations to more complete submissions. The lower allocation reflects missing evidence as well as lower merit. It does not establish that every excluded program lacks value.

**Reduced change capacity.** Reduce Change and Sustain coverage to 60%, while retaining mandatory coverage and the other reservations. Use this to assess the consequences of a capacity reduction. The current source does not establish that all service outcomes can be maintained at this level.

**Reservation settings.** The regional and hygiene percentages are starting allocations for the three scenarios. Adjust them as owners confirm demand and scope. Regional coverage includes allocations made in earlier stages. The hygiene reservation also includes qualifying work already funded as a protected obligation.

**Where the recommended allocation goes.** Protected obligations **27.796 M€**; Change and Sustain **22.921 M€**; additional regional programs **0.900 M€**; carry-overs **11.823 M€**; additional hygiene work **7.162 M€**; common demand **23.658 M€**. These amounts add to **94.260 M€**. Hygiene coverage totals **7.562 M€**, including **0.400 M€** already funded under protected obligations.

**Regional overlap.** Remaining regional capacity equals the regional allowance less all earlier regional allocations, with a minimum of zero. If a 5.700 M€ allowance already includes 2.000 M€ of protected regional demand, only **3.700 M€** remains. In the current Balanced investment scenario, the **5.525 M€** allowance includes **4.302 M€** of protected obligations and **0.308 M€** of Change and Sustain. That leaves **0.915 M€** for additional demand. Whole-program selection uses **0.900 M€**, leaving **0.015 M€**. If protected allocations exceed the regional allowance, show the excess and stop additional regional funding.

**Decision implications.** Confirmed evidence funds 54 fewer requests than Balanced investment. Reduced change capacity releases **9.168 M€** from Change and Sustain. It creates room for one additional **0.080 M€** regional request because earlier regional capacity consumption falls. The higher request count therefore does not mean greater delivery capacity. Balanced investment remains the recommended starting point.

**Outstanding evidence.** Balanced investment includes 78 funded requests with Unknown waste assessments. Review these conditions, the Group Functions and Supply Chain split, and reduced-scope decisions before final approval. The model preserves all 506 source records, including records without a positive request. Figures are planning recommendations based on the submitted information.
