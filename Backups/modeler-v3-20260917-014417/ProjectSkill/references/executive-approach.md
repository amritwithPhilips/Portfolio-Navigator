# Annual Operating Plan 2027

## Executive recommendation

Use `15926_IT AOP2027 Demand.xlsx` as the sole evidence and calculation source. It contains 279.595 MEUR of requested investment across 509 demand-evidence rows: 111.699 MEUR enterprise-wide and 167.896 MEUR business-led.

Protect the source P1 and P2 MUST-DO decisions and the three approved CCB exceptions in LFR. This creates an LFR baseline of 34.517 MEUR: 16.502 MEUR from 33 source P1/P2 decisions plus Group Functions 7.630 MEUR, Supply Chain 4.160 MEUR, and Finance 6.225 MEUR.

Use Sustain Engineering as the visible label and include only source rows explicitly classified `Change & Sustain`. After removing the three LFR exceptions, its source base is 12.333 MEUR. Hygiene then contains only the approved decommissioning, continuous-improvement, upgrade and enhancement categories. Carryover requires numeric benefits and a configurable 1x to 5x+ ROI threshold, default 2x. ROI-Based Portfolio automatically receives remaining AOP capacity.

LFR, Sustain Engineering and Hygiene each support `% of bucket demand` or `% of Total AOP`. Fund whole initiatives in ranked order with a default 5% automatic tolerance. Conference inclusions sit on top of the prioritized selection and may make the final scenario over budget; show the variance and rationale rather than blocking the decision.

## Decision evidence

Use the source Decision band and Overall AOP decision, not a recalculation of the nine waste dimensions. The source has 229 populated Decision bands covering 128.406 MEUR: 215 Healthy at 124.406 MEUR and 14 Contain at 4.000 MEUR. Another 280 rows totaling 151.189 MEUR are unassessed.

The Overall AOP decision records 183 Healthy-ranked requests at 108.154 MEUR, 30 P1 Immediate at 15.402 MEUR, three P2 Scheduled at 1.100 MEUR, 12 Contain at 3.250 MEUR, one MUST-DO priority-TBD at 0.500 MEUR, and 280 blank at 151.189 MEUR.

## Merit and traceability

Use a 100-point merit score: submitted benefit relative to requested investment 50, submitted priority 25, Captured Consequence 15, and source Decision band 10. Score Captured Consequence only from Impact and Cost of Not Doing, Problem Statement, and Scope. Retain the supporting passages and source row.

The source has at least one of those three fields populated on 425 evidence rows covering 245.515 MEUR; all three are populated on 198 rows covering 117.953 MEUR. Missing evidence remains visible and receives no invented points.

The Excel model holds the source mapping, normalized records, calculations, settings, decisions, explanations and scenario version. The dashboard uses the same records and engine so every total and program decision traces back to the Excel model and original source workbook. Finalise freezes the scenario; Refresh creates a new version.
