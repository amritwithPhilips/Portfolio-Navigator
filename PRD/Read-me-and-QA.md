## Deliverables

- **AOP2027-Prioritisation-PRD.pdf**: 18-page requirements document covering the recommended funding sequence, levers, scoring, seven scenarios, BU and Function Conference flows, workbook requirements, governance and acceptance criteria.
- **AOP2027-Prioritisation-PRD.md**: editable text of the same document.
- **AOP2027-Scored-Demand.xlsx**: seven tabs with 506 retained records, 481 named demands scored under both frameworks, source evidence, configurable scoring and conference inputs.

Start with the executive recommendation and sections 3–7 of the PRD. In the workbook, open **Overview**, then compare **Scored demand** and **Score settings**. Yellow inputs accept reviewed scope, benefits, expected loss, impact and conference decisions. Source snapshots in **Demand details** remain the audit reference; updating the source register requires a reconciled refresh.

## Interpretation

The Reference profile preserves the submitted five-dimensional framework, with an explicit configurable Cost avoidance mapping. The Recommended profile adds impact and cost of not doing and removes points for missing evidence. Scores are provisional evidence-based comparisons, not funding approvals. The workbook does not implement the future allocation engine or live cross-tab conference workflow.

The 168.000 M€ planning envelope, 10% Region reservation, scoring weights and scenario settings are recommendations for review. LFR and service reservation percentages require confirmed minimum amounts. No unsupported monetary loss or approved benefit values have been invented. Numeric monetary columns use M€; original narratives retain their supplied wording and units.
