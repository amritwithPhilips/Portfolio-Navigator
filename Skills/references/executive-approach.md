## Four-priority classification (eligible-only buckets)

P1 (Legal and Compliance): Only LFRs with compliance name AND year, plus three CCB exceptions. LFRs without evidence go to P4.

P2 (High-Value Carry-Overs): Only carry-overs with a confirmed baselined business case AND Must Do/Must Have priority. Non-baselined carry-overs go to P4. Funding capped at the original business case amount.

P3 (Sustain Engineering and Hygiene): Change & Sustain demand funded first, then Continuous Improvement and Upgrades & Enhancements ranked by merit score.

P4 (Value Based Portfolio): All remaining demand including new features, AI, decommissioning, plus LFRs without evidence and carry-overs without baseline. These compete on merit.

## Decision flow

Three scenario types: Default (data-backed only), Leadership (captures BU preferences), Conference (consolidated final position after sign-off).
